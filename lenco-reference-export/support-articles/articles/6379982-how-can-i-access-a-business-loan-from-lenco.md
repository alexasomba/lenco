# How can I access a business loan from Lenco?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6379982-how-can-i-access-a-business-loan-from-lenco)

You must have a Lenco account which should have been operated for
a minimum of 90days. If you are yet to have an account, you can
sign up here:
[https://lenco.co/signup](https://lenco.co/signup)

As soon as you are eligible for a business Loan, you would see
your credit limit on your Lenco dashboard.

We would be unable to grant you a credit that is above your limit.
It would help if you run more of your business financial
operations on Lenco. This will help you access a higher credit
limit.
