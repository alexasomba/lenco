# Why should I upload my ID and BVN?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000505-why-should-i-upload-my-id-and-bvn)

Uploading your ID and BVN helps us verify the identity of our
customers for KYC purposes. ID and BVN are both vital for the
opening of a bank account.
