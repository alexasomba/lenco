What is the difference between a Direct and Prepaid Card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article

About the different types of Lenco cards and their uses.

Direct Card: This is linked directly to your Lenco account. Transactions made with a direct card are deducted directly from your business account.

Prepaid Card: This is not linked directly to your Lenco account. This can be pre-funded, such that you fund the card only with the amount needed and it doesn't debit directly from your business account.
