# How long does it take to open a Lenco account?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000088-how-long-does-it-take-to-open-a-lenco-account)

We will create a Lenco account within 24-48 hours for you
**after** you have provided all the required documents.
​
Please
provide all information and documentation as accurately as
possible to avoid delays.
