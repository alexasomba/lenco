# What is Lenco?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000190-what-is-lenco)

Lenco is a digital-only bank that makes opening and operating a
free current bank account easy for businesses, Startups and SMEs.
Hassle-free.

We currently fully operate in three countries: Nigeria as
[Lenco](https://lenco.co), Zambia as
[Lenco by BroadPay](https://www.broadpayzm.com/)
and Malawi and we are going live in
[Kenya](https://lenco.co/ke)
and
[Rwanda](https://lenco.co/rw)
soon.
