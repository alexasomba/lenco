How can I activate my Lenco card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article

To activate the cards, kindly download the updated Lenco App.

To activate your card, kindly follow these steps:

  • Log into your Lenco account.

  • Click on Cards.

  • Click on Activate Card and follow the instructions.

After activating the card, you will be given a default PIN which you can change at the nearest ATM. Then, check your account balance with the new PIN to confirm that your PIN has been changed.

NB: Where you have team members, only admins can activate the card.
