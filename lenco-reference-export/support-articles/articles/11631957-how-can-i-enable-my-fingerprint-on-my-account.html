How can I enable my Fingerprint on my account?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article

Enabling fingerprint helps a customer to login to their account with ease and on the go without having to check their mail.

To enable your fingerprint on your account, kindly follow the steps below;

  • Login to the App

  • Click on More

  • Clcik on Profile

  • Click on Security Centre

  • Then, click on Enable Fingerprint
