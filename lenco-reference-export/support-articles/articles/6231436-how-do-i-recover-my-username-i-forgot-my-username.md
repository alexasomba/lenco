# How do I recover my username? I forgot my username

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6231436-how-do-i-recover-my-username-i-forgot-my-username)

To recover your username, you can check your email for an email
from Lenco with the subject
**Your email address has been verified**. You will find your
username in that email.
​
If you have forgotten your
password, use this link to recover your password:
[https://app.lenco.co/forgot-password](https://app.lenco.co/forgot-password)
