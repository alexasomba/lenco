# How can I turn off SMS alerts?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/11079780-how-can-i-turn-off-sms-alerts)

To turn off SMS alerts:

- Kindly log in to the App
- Click on **More**
- Then **Profile**
- Click on **Notification**
- And **turn off SMS**
