Who can get the Lenco Terminal (POS)?

Collection: Lenco Terminals (POS)

Original article

Preferably, you need to be a registered business to get the Lenco Terminal (POS). However, if you run a business with the need to do collections with a Terminal (POS), you can get Lenco Terminal whether you have a bank account with Lenco or not.

You can place an order for the Terminal (POS) before applying for a business account, and your Terminal (POS) will be shipped once you complete your order requirements.
