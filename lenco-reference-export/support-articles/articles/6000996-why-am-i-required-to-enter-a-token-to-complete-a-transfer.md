# Why am I required to enter a token to complete a transfer?

Collection: Making and receiving payments

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000996-why-am-i-required-to-enter-a-token-to-complete-a-transfer)

You will be required to put in a token to complete a transfer.
This is an extra layer of security for your account. To generate
quick tokens for account operations, click
[https://lenco.co/security](https://lenco.co/security)
to set up your token app.
