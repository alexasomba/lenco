# How to verify your email after sign up

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6383137-how-to-verify-your-email-after-sign-up)

We sent an email to you with the title:
**Verify your email address**

Search your email for the "Verify your email address", and you
will find the link to click and verify your email address.

If not, please email
[support@lenco.co](mailto:support@lenco.co), and you will be sent a verification email.
