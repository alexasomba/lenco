# How do I fund the card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8812866-how-do-i-fund-the-card)

To fund the card:

- Kindly login to the App.
- Click on 'Cards'**.**
- Then, click on 'Physical Card'.
- Select 'Fund Card’ and proceed with your request.
