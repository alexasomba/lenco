How can I change my password?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article

Passwords should be kept safe and changed once they have been compromised.

To change your password, follow the steps below:

Option 1: Kindly reset your password using this link

Option 2:

  • On your login page, click on Forgot your password?

  • Enter your Email address.

  • Then, click on Reset your password.

The reset link will be sent to your mail.

Option 3:

Alternatively,

  • Click on SETTINGS on the homepage

  • Click on SECURITY

  • Click on CHANGE PASSWORD
