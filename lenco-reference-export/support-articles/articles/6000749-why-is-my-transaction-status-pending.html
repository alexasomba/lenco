Why is my transaction status Pending?

Collection: Making and receiving payments

Original article

A transaction status can be shown as pending on your dashboard

A transaction status can be shown as pending on your dashboard when:

  • The transaction is not yet approved by the designated account approver.

  • The transaction is being reviewed by our anti-fraud unit.

  • The transaction is yet to be processed.

  • There is an issue with the recipient bank.

  • There is a downtime on the platform.
