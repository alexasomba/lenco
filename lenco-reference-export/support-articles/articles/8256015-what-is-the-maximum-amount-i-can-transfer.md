# What is the maximum amount I can transfer?

Collection: Making and receiving payments

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8256015-what-is-the-maximum-amount-i-can-transfer)

Our policy for the daily transfer limit for unregistered
businesses is N500,000.

However, businesses registered with CAC have a daily transfer
limit of N1M - N20M.

To view your daily transfer limit, kindly
[log in](https://app.lenco.co/login)
to your dashboard, then click on **Account.**

Please note that you can request to increase your limit as we
learn more about your business.

NB: There is no limit to the inflow on the account.
