What is the daily transaction limit on the card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article

The amount you can payout daily using the expense card.

The maximum amount you can transfer daily is;

Direct Debit: the transaction limit in your Lenco account.

Prepaid: limited to the amount funded on the card.

Please note that our policy for the daily transfer limit for unregistered businesses is N500,000.

However, businesses registered with CAC have a daily transfer limit of N10M - N20M.

PS: You can request to increase your limit as we learn more about your business.
