# What is the settlement process for Lenco by BroadPay?

Collection: Lenco by BroadPay

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8229422-what-is-the-settlement-process-for-lenco-by-broadpay)

Kindly see the settlement process below.

- Mobile Money - Instant
- Local Cards - T+1 (Next day)
- International Cards - T+8

Please note that the merchant is responsible for covering bank
fees and transfer charges.
