# How much is the Lenco card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8592269-how-much-is-the-lenco-card)

You will be charged a fee of N1000 for your card request (minimum
₦2,000 bank account balance required).

For Registered business, you can order your first card free of
charge (minimum ₦2,000 bank account balance required). However,
you will be charged a fee of N1000 for subsequent card requests.
P.S: You can request more than one card for one business.

Delivery is free. However, you will be charged a fee for every
re-attempted delivery where you are not available to receive your
Card.

[Apply](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6231654-request-lenco-cards)
for a card today and get it delivered to you within 72hrs.
