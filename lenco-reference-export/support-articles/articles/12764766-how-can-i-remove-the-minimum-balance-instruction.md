# How can I remove the minimum balance instruction?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/12764766-how-can-i-remove-the-minimum-balance-instruction)

To remove the Minimum Balance Instruction in your account, kindly
follow the steps below:

- Log in to the **Web**
- Click on **SETTINGS** on the home page.
- Click on **POLICIES**
- Click on **BALANCE INSTRUCTIONS**
- Click on **MINIMUM BALANCE**to remove the restriction.
