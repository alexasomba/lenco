# How can I delete a saved recipient?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8480078-how-can-i-delete-a-saved-recipient)

To delete a saved recipient;

- Log in via web [https://lenco.co/](https://lenco.co/)
- Click on Payments.
- Click on Recipient.
- On the list of payment recipients, check for the recipient you want to delete.
- Then select the three dots on your right to delete.
