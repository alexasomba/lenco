# How can I remove a team member from sub-accounts?

Collection: Team Access

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000668-how-can-i-remove-a-team-member-from-sub-accounts)

To remove a team member from a sub-account, follow the steps
below;

- Click on **SETTINGS**on the home page.
- Click on **TEAM MEMBERS.**
- Click on the team member to be removed.
- Click **REMOVE** on any of the sub-accounts.
