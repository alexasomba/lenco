# How can I remove the restriction to send money?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/12765202-how-can-i-remove-the-restriction-to-send-money)

To remove the restriction to send money, kindly follow the steps
below:

- Log in to the **Web**
- Click on **SETTINGS** on the home page.
- Click on **POLICIES**
- Click on **ACCOUNT RESTRICTIONS**
- Click on **SEND MONEY RULE**and click on Send Money To Any Account.
