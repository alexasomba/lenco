# How can I delete a sub-account?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/10532052-how-can-i-delete-a-sub-account)

Currently, sub-accounts cannot be deleted but can be renamed.

To rename a sub-account, kindly send the request as an email to
[supports@lenco.co](mailto:supports@lenco.co)
