# How can I login into my Lenco account?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6424019-how-can-i-login-into-my-lenco-account)

- Visit [https://app.lenco.co](https://app.lenco.co)
- Log in with either your email address or username (sent to you during your account opening) and password.
- After which, you enter the OTP sent to your email or the OTP generated from the Lenco Token App.
