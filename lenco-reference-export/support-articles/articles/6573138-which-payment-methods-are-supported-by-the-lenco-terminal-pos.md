# Which payment methods are supported by the Lenco Terminal (POS)?

Collection: Lenco Terminals (POS)

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6573138-which-payment-methods-are-supported-by-the-lenco-terminal-pos)

The Lenco Terminal (POS) supports all Chip & Pin Visa,
Mastercard and Verve payments regardless of the bank or provider
that issued them.
