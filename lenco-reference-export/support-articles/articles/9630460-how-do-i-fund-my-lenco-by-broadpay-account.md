# How do I fund my Lenco by BroadPay account?

Collection: Lenco by BroadPay

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/9630460-how-do-i-fund-my-lenco-by-broadpay-account)

You can fund your Lenco by BroadPay account with bank transfers or
mobile money.

To get your account number:

- Kindly login to your Lenco dashboard
- Then Click on 'Add money' to view your account details.
