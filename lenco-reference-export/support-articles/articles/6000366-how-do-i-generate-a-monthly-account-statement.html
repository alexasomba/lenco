How do I generate a monthly account statement?

Collection: General

Original article

Kindly send a request to support@lenco.co with the period in which you want the statement and it will be delivered to your mailbox.

To generate an account statement, follow the steps below;
​
​Option 1:

Send an email with a request to [email protected]

For Zambian Merchants, kindly send an email with a request to [email protected]
​
​Option 2:

  • Click on TRANSACTIONS on the homepage

  • Filter by DATES, CATEGORIES, RECIPIENTS or OTHERS

  • Click on APPLY FILTER

  • Click on EXPORT (PDF or CSV) to download the statement.
