# How can I edit my company information?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000443-how-can-i-edit-my-company-information)

To edit your company information, follow the steps below;

- Click on **SETTINGS** on the homepage
- Click on any of the icons (**account settings, security, team members**) to edit.
