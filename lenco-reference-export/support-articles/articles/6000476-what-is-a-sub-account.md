# What is a Sub-account?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000476-what-is-a-sub-account)

A sub-account is another account you can create under your primary
account to help you better manage your business funds and
accounting.

For example, you can create a sub-account for specific
departments, products and services of the business and can help
you manage different business outlets (branches) by assigning a
specific sub-account to each outlet.

You can create an unlimited number of sub-accounts. We generate
the sub-account numbers instantly and they are free to use.
