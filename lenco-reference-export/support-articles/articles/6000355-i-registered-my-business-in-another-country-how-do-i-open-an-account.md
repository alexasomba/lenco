# I registered my business in another country. How do I open an account?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000355-i-registered-my-business-in-another-country-how-do-i-open-an-account)

If your business is registered in Nigeria, Zambia, Malawi or
Kenya, you can apply for a Lenco business bank account.

This account lets you manage your business finances easily and
securely.

You can access your account conveniently online or through the
Lenco App from anywhere in the world.
