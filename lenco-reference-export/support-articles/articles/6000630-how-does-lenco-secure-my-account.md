# How does Lenco secure my account?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000630-how-does-lenco-secure-my-account)

We secured your Lenco account as we warehouse all your funds with
our partner banks like Providus Bank Plc. Also, we insure our
customers' funds with the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation
(NDIC).
