# What is the daily transaction limit on the card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8813214-what-is-the-daily-transaction-limit-on-the-card)

The maximum amount you can transfer daily is;

**Direct Debit:**the transaction limit in your Lenco account.

**Prepaid:** limited to the amount funded on the card.

Please note that our policy for the daily transfer limit for
unregistered businesses is N500,000.

However, businesses registered with CAC have a daily transfer
limit of N10M - N20M.

PS: You can request to increase your limit as we learn more about
your business.
