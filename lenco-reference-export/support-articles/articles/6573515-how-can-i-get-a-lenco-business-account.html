How can I get a Lenco business account?

Collection: General

Original article

I want to open a Lenco business account.

Nigeria

For your Nigerian business account, kindly complete the following steps:

  • Signup.

  • Verify your email.

  • Fill out the application form and submit the required documents.

  • Wait for your application to be reviewed.

Your account application will be approved in 24-48 working hours, you will be sent an email containing your account number.
