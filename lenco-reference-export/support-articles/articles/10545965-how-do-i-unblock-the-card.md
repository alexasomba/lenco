# How do I unblock the card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/10545965-how-do-i-unblock-the-card)

**Option 1:**To unfreeze your Card, follow the steps below:

- Kindly login to your Lenco App.
- Click on 'Cards'.
- Then, click on 'Unfreeze Card'.

**Option 2:**If the Card was blocked as a result of entering
the wrong PIN thrice, kindly send a mail to
[cardservices@lenco.co](mailto:cardservices@lenco.co)
