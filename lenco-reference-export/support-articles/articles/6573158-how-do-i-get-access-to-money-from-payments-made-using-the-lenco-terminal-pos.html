How do I get access to money from payments made using the Lenco Terminal (POS)?

Collection: Lenco Terminals (POS)

Original article

Funds should appear in your Lenco account or any other account you provided in less than 24 hours after payment.
​
If you are receiving via your Lenco account, you can find all your transactions in your account tab on the dashboard of your Lenco App. Then you can select make a transfer and transfer your funds as you want to.
