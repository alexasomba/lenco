# What do I do when my transaction status is showing as pending?

Collection: Making and receiving payments

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000972-what-do-i-do-when-my-transaction-status-is-showing-as-pending)

Your transaction can pend for several reasons. When experiencing
this, kindly hold on with initiating the transaction again, until
the pending transaction has been resolved as either
**successful** or **failed.**
