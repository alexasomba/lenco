How can I add a team member?

Collection: Team Access

Original article

With team banking you can add your team members, define access levels, notification rule and the type of notification they will receive.

To add a team member;

  • Click on Settings from the dashboard.

  • Click on Team Members.

  • Then click on Invite A Team Member.

The team member invited would receive an email with the link to join your team on Lenco.

Then select the command at the top that says “Create An Account".
