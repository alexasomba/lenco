What is the maximum amount I can transfer?

Collection: Making and receiving payments

Original article

Our policy for the daily transfer limit for unregistered businesses is N500,000.

However, businesses registered with CAC have a daily transfer limit of N1M - N20M.

To view your daily transfer limit, kindly log in to your dashboard, then click on Account.

Please note that you can request to increase your limit as we learn more about your business.

NB: There is no limit to the inflow on the account.
