# How can I change my Account / Business name?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/10550588-how-can-i-change-my-account-business-name)

To change your account name, kindly send your updated CAC
documents as an email to
[supports@lenco.co](mailto:supports@lenco.co).

For Zambian Merchants, kindly send an email to
[support.zm@lenco.co](mailto:support.zm@lenco.co).
