# How can I get my default card PIN?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/10205482-how-can-i-get-my-default-card-pin)

To get your default PIN:

- Kindly login to the App.
- Click on 'Cards'.
- Click on the three dots.
- Then click on 'Request Default PIN'
- Select 'Show PIN'
