How much is the Lenco card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article

You will be charged a fee of N1000 for your card request (minimum ₦2,000 bank account balance required).

For Registered business, you can order your first card free of charge (minimum ₦2,000 bank account balance required). However, you will be charged a fee of N1000 for subsequent card requests. P.S: You can request more than one card for one business.

Delivery is free. However, you will be charged a fee for every re-attempted delivery where you are not available to receive your Card.
