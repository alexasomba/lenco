How can I link my Token app with my account?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article

The token app is very essential because it helps a customer have quick access to token, for them to sign into their account with ease and on the go without having to check their mail.

To link your token app with your account, follow the steps below;

  1. Login to the App

  • Click on More

  • Click on Profile

  • Click on Security Centre

  • Then, click on Token App

  • Click on Setup Token App

2. Login to the Web

  • Click on SETTINGS on the homepage

  • Click on SECURITY

  • Click on SETUP TOKEN APP
