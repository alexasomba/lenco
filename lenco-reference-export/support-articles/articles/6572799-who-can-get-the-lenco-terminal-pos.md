# Who can get the Lenco Terminal (POS)?

Collection: Lenco Terminals (POS)

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6572799-who-can-get-the-lenco-terminal-pos)

Preferably, you need to be a registered business to get the Lenco
Terminal (POS). However, if you run a business with the need to do
collections with a Terminal (POS), you can get Lenco Terminal
whether you have a bank account with Lenco or not.

You can place an
[order](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6557774-how-do-i-order-a-lenco-terminal-pos)
for the Terminal (POS) before applying for a business account, and
your Terminal (POS) will be shipped once you complete your order
requirements.
