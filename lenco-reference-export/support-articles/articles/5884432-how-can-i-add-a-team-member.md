# How can I add a team member?

Collection: Team Access

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/5884432-how-can-i-add-a-team-member)

To add a team member;

- Click on **Settings** from the dashboard.
- Click on **Team Members**.
- Then click on **Invite A Team Member.**

The team member invited would receive an email with the link to
join your team on Lenco.

Then select the command at the top that says “**Create An Account**".
