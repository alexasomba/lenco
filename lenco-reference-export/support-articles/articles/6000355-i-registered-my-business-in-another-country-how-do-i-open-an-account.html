I registered my business in another country. How do I open an account?

Collection: General

Original article

You can open a Lenco account for your business registered in a country other than Nigeria.

If your business is registered in Nigeria, Zambia, Malawi or Kenya, you can apply for a Lenco business bank account.

This account lets you manage your business finances easily and securely.

You can access your account conveniently online or through the Lenco App from anywhere in the world.
