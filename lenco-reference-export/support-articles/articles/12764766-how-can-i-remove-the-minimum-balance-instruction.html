How can I remove the minimum balance instruction?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article

You can remove the minimum balance instruction from the account. Once you do, your account will no longer have a minimum balance restriction.

To remove the Minimum Balance Instruction in your account, kindly follow the steps below:

  • Log in to the Web

  • Click on SETTINGS on the home page.

  • Click on POLICIES

  • Click on BALANCE INSTRUCTIONS

  • Click on MINIMUM BALANCE to remove the restriction.
