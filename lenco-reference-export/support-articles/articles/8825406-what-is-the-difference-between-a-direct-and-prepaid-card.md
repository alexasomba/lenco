# What is the difference between a Direct and Prepaid Card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8825406-what-is-the-difference-between-a-direct-and-prepaid-card)

**Direct Card:** This is linked directly to your Lenco account.
Transactions made with a direct card are deducted directly from
your business account.

**Prepaid Card:** This is not linked directly to your Lenco
account. This can be pre-funded, such that you fund the card only
with the amount needed and it doesn't debit directly from your
business account.
