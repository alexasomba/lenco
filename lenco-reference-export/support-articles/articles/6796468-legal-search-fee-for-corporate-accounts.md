# Legal Search Fee for Corporate Accounts.

Collection: Fee

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6796468-legal-search-fee-for-corporate-accounts)

To issue a corporate bank account in Nigeria, it is required that
the bank carry out a search on the company. As such, the process
comes at a fee.

The legal search fee is a statutory fee levied by banks for legal
search on businesses for account opening. The search is a
compulsory requirement by the regulator(CBN) for corporate account
opening.

For more details on the fee, click
[here](https://lenco.co/pricing)

For Lenco customers, the fee applies to Prime accounts and it does
not delay account opening. It is usually charged between 1-6
months after the account has been opened. Please note that it is a
one-time charge.
