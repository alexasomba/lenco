# How can I enable my Fingerprint on my account?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/11631957-how-can-i-enable-my-fingerprint-on-my-account)

To enable your fingerprint on your account, kindly follow the
steps below;

- Login to the App
- Click on **More**
- Clcik on **Profile**
- Click on **Security Centre**
- Then, click on **Enable Fingerprint**
