# Can I make an International payment with the Lenco Card?

Collection: Lenco Cards

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8825613-can-i-make-an-international-payment-with-the-lenco-card)

The Lenco Card is acceptable across global and international
platforms like Flywire, Facebook, Netflix, Showmax, Spotify, Uber,
Microsoft, Emirates, and Amazon Prime.
