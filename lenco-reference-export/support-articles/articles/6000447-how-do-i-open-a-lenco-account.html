How do I open a Lenco account?

Collection: General

Original article

You can open a Lenco account by visiting lenco.co to Sign up, Select Country and Get Started.

Alternatively, you can reach out directly to us via call or WhatsApp on our helpline at +2348094278928 for more help.

For Zambian Merchants, you can reach out directly to us via Email or our helpline at +260978948474 for more help.
