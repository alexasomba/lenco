How can I get the Lenco Token App?

Collection: Security & Privacy

Original article

For tokens and OTP to securely access your account we send OTPs to your email or you can use OTP provided by your Token App

There are two ways you can receive OTPs:

  1. Via email: You can get OTP via email when you log in or make transactions.

  2. Via the Lenco Token App: you can setup the Lenco Token App on your phone, which will automatically generate OTPs for you each time you need it.

    1. Download the Lenco Token App for Android.

    2. Download the Lenco Token App for iOS.
