# How do I get access to money from payments made using the Lenco Terminal (POS)?

Collection: Lenco Terminals (POS)

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6573158-how-do-i-get-access-to-money-from-payments-made-using-the-lenco-terminal-pos)

Funds should appear in your Lenco account or any other account you
provided in less than 24 hours after payment.
​
If you
are receiving via your Lenco account, you can find all your
transactions in your account tab on the dashboard of your
[Lenco App](https://lenco.app.link/home). Then you can select make a transfer and transfer your funds as
you want to.
