# Is there a limit to the amount of money I can have in my account?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000340-is-there-a-limit-to-the-amount-of-money-i-can-have-in-my-account)

There is no limit to the amount a customer can have in their
account. Customers can fund their accounts with as much money as
they want.
