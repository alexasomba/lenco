# Is there a limit to the number of accounts I can have?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000064-is-there-a-limit-to-the-number-of-accounts-i-can-have)

Each business is eligible to have one account. You can create
multiple sub-accounts to manage the different needs of your
business.

You can also open an account for as many registered businesses as
you have.
