# Is Lenco regulated?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/6000339-is-lenco-regulated)

Lenco is a CBN licensed Payment Solutions Service Provider.
Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulates our partner bank (Providus
Bank Plc) and Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) insures
all funds.
