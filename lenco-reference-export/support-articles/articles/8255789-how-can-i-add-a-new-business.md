# How can I add a new business?

Collection: General

Original article: [view source](https://support.lenco.co/en/articles/8255789-how-can-i-add-a-new-business)

To add a new business;

- Log in via web [https://lenco.co/](https://lenco.co/)
- Click on the drop-down arrow beside the account number
- Click on “Add new business” and upload all relevant documents and details.
